Where one call is the table

Two prompts from this set, two generic examples, and two in a customer's shape. Exa and Parallel return ranked links. They find a page. A table whose rows live on different pages is the SELECT job: one SQL call, not a loop of three searches.

FromThe questionWhy a short search loop stops
ExampleList every orbital launch attempt by a Chinese rocket in September 2026, with the date and the vehicle.The rows are a month of launches, not one headline.
ExampleWhich countries signed the Artemis Accords between 1 January and 30 September 2026? List them in order of signing.Seventeen signings, each its own announcement.
SpaceXEvery special temporary authority granted to SpaceX Services for the Hillsboro and Atlanta gateways in 2026: file number, grant date, term and basis.Eight rows across four weekly FCC notices. A snippet shows one.
SpaceXFor Guernsey, Jersey, Cayman, Turks and Caicos and the Falklands: Starlink's licensee entity, licence class, date and one distinctive condition.Five regulators, five documents, no roundup page.
Any customerFor each of the six largest US banks: Q2 net interest income, the credit provision, and the 10-Q URL.Three searches cover three filings.
Any customerSeries A rounds in European legal-AI companies announced this year: company, lead, amount, and the announcement from the company or the investor.Three searches return the same two news posts.

The same table, as of a date

Search takes query_time and reads the index as it stood then. Exa can bound the same question with start_published_date and end_published_date, or pin a stored page with snapshotAsOf. Parallel can set after_date. A list that already sits on one page is still a lookup: fetch that page. These rows are the job. They are not a score on this audit.
